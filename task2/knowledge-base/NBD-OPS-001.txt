# Heavy Equipment Inventory and Maintenance Schedule

## 1. Purpose
To document equipment inventory counts and the standardized maintenance interval policy.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all NBD-owned heavy equipment used on active sites.

## 3. Core Information
NBD tracks heavy equipment inventory and performs scheduled maintenance based on operating hours.
Maintenance logs must be recorded per asset.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Cranes: 14
Excavators: 22
Loaders: 18
Maintenance Interval: every 250 operating hours
Maintenance Log Format: EQ-YYYY-XXX

## 5. Reference Codes
OPS-EQ-250H
LOG-EQ-YYYY-XXX
