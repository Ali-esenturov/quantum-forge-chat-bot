# Expense Structure and Operational Costs

## 1. Purpose
To describe the expense distribution model used for internal cost reporting.

## 2. Scope
Applies to operational cost planning and margin analysis for projects.

## 3. Core Information
Costs are grouped into material, labor, equipment, and administrative categories.
Percentages represent typical distribution for 2025.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Material Costs: 41%
Labor Costs: 34%
Equipment Costs: 17%
Administrative Costs: 8%
Target Operating Margin Range: 12%–18%
