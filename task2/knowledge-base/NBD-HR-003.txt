# Training and Certification Requirements

## 1. Purpose
To define mandatory training and certification intervals for employees.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all employees and site-access contractors (training equivalence required).

## 3. Core Information
Safety training is mandatory and repeated regularly.
Engineering licenses must be renewed according to policy.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Mandatory Safety Training: 2 times per year
Engineering License Renewal: every 3 years
New Hire Safety Induction: within first 5 working days
Training Record System: TrainLog (internal)

## 5. Reference Codes
HR-TRAIN-2X
LIC-RENEW-3Y

training requirements, safety training, certification, license renewal, induction
