# Emergency Response and Crisis Management Plan

## 1. Purpose
To define emergency response procedures and drill requirements.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all sites, offices, and project locations operated by NBD.

## 3. Core Information
Emergency plans include evacuation, medical response, and incident command roles.
Regular drills ensure readiness.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Evacuation Time Target: under 7 minutes
Emergency Drill Frequency: quarterly
Crisis Response Code: CR-NBD-07
Emergency Contact Tree Update: every 6 months
