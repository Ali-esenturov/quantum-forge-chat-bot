# Environmental Compliance and Sustainability Standards

## 1. Purpose
To define environmental requirements and sustainability targets for projects.

## 2. Scope
Applies to procurement, materials usage, waste handling, and emissions tracking.

## 3. Core Information
NBD targets reduced emissions and increased recycled material use.
Environmental compliance must align with recognized standards.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
CO2 Emission Reduction Target: 18%
Recycled Material Usage Target: 22%
Certification Target: ISO 14001 alignment
Waste Segregation Categories: 4 (metal, concrete, wood, mixed)

## 5. Reference Codes
ECO-ISO14001
RECYC-22P

## 6. Keywords
environmental compliance, sustainability, CO2 reduction, recycled materials, ISO 14001
