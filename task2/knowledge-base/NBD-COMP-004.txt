# Internal Audit Procedures

## 1. Purpose
To define the internal audit schedule, scoring, and corrective action workflow.

## 2. Scope
Applies to safety audits, contractor audits, and process compliance audits.

## 3. Core Information
Audits are conducted by CSO using standardized checklists.
Each audit results in a compliance score and corrective actions.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Audit Frequency: every 30 days (per active site)
Audit Team Size: 3 members
Compliance Score Threshold: 85%
Corrective Action Due Window: 14 days
