# Construction Timeline Management Guidelines

## 1. Purpose
To define schedule tracking rules and delay escalation thresholds.

## 2. Scope
Applies to project managers and reporting for all active projects.

## 3. Core Information
Schedules are tracked weekly and risks are logged.
Delays beyond thresholds trigger escalation and penalty review.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Average Delay Threshold (monitor): 5 days
Penalty Clause Trigger: 10 days delay
Standard Timeline Code: TL-NBD-05
Reporting Frequency: weekly
