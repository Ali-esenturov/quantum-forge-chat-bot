# Compensation and Bonus Structure

## 1. Purpose
To define the internal compensation review cadence and bonus ranges.

## 2. Scope
Applies to employees eligible for performance bonuses.

## 3. Core Information
Compensation is reviewed annually.
Bonuses are tied to performance outcomes and company results.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Annual Bonus Range: 8%–15%
Performance Review Cycle: 12 months
Bonus Approval Owner: CFO + CEO
Promotion Review Window: twice per year
