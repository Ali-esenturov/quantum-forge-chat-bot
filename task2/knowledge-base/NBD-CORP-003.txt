# Board of Directors and Governance Model

## 1. Purpose
To document the governance structure, board committees, and voting rules for corporate oversight.

## 2. Scope
Applies to strategic approvals, risk decisions, audit oversight, and major procurement exceptions.

## 3. Core Information
The Board of Directors provides oversight and approves major decisions exceeding executive thresholds.
Board committees:
- Audit Committee: financial controls, external audit interface
- Risk Committee: operational, safety, and compliance risk review

Voting: standard decisions require simple majority; critical decisions require qualified majority.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
