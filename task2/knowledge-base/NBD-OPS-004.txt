# Quality Assurance and Inspection Process

## 1. Purpose
To define QA inspection cadence and defect thresholds for NBD projects.

## 2. Scope
Applies to site inspections, punch lists, and quality reporting.

## 3. Core Information
Quality is validated through scheduled inspections and defect tracking.
Non-conformities require corrective actions and re-inspection.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Inspection Frequency: weekly
Defect Tolerance Rate: below 2%
QA Compliance Benchmark: 96%
Re-inspection Window: 7 days after correction

## 5. Reference Codes
QA-WEEKLY-2P
QA-BENCH-96
