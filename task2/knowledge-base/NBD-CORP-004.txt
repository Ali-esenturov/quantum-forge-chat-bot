# Organizational Department Structure

## 1. Purpose
To define internal departments, reporting responsibilities, and the high-level organizational map.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all internal documentation referencing departments or functional ownership.

## 3. Core Information
NordBuild Dynamics is organized into four primary divisions/offices:
1) Structural Engineering Division (SED)
2) Infrastructure & Roads Division (IRD)
3) Urban Development Division (UDD)
4) Compliance & Safety Office (CSO)

SED focuses on structural design standards and engineering approvals.
IRD focuses on roads, bridges, utilities, and infrastructure delivery.
UDD focuses on urban commercial and residential development execution.
CSO focuses on safety audits, compliance checks, and incident investigations.
