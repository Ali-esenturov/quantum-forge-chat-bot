# Construction Site Safety Standards

## 1. Purpose
To define baseline safety requirements applicable to all construction sites.

## 2. Scope
Applies to employees, contractors, and visitors at any NBD-controlled site.

## 3. Core Information
Mandatory PPE: helmet, safety boots, gloves, high-visibility vest.
Daily safety briefing is required before work begins.
Heavy machinery zones must be clearly marked and controlled.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Daily Briefing Time: 08:00–08:15
Minimum Safety Radius (heavy machinery): 15 meters
Safety Officer: required per site, per shift
Violation Severity Scale: S1–S4 (see NBD-COMP-003)
