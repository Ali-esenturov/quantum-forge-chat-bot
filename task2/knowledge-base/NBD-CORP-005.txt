# Corporate Mission, Vision, and Strategic Goals

## 1. Purpose
To state the mission, vision, and measurable strategic objectives of NBD.

## 2. Scope
Applies to strategic planning, annual objectives, and executive reporting.

## 3. Core Information
Mission: deliver reliable, safe, and sustainable construction outcomes that improve urban living.
Vision: become a top-5 mid-size construction provider in the EU by 2030.

Strategic goals:
- Improve safety performance year over year
- Standardize engineering and QA across all sites
- Expand infrastructure portfolio with predictable margins

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Target Revenue (2030): €150,000,000
Target Safety Incident Reduction: 20% by 2028
Strategic Horizon: 2026–2030
