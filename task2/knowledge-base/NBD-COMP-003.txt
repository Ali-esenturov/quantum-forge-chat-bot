# Incident Classification and Reporting Framework

## 1. Purpose
To define incident severity levels and reporting timelines.

## 2. Scope
Applies to safety incidents, equipment failures, and compliance violations.

## 3. Core Information
Incidents are classified using four levels:
S1 Minor
S2 Moderate
S3 Major
S4 Critical

All incidents require an initial report and assigned owner.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Reporting Deadline: within 24 hours of discovery
Mandatory Fields: site, time, category, severity, immediate actions
Escalation: S3–S4 must be escalated to CSO within 2 hours

## 5. Reference Codes
INC-SCALE-S1S4
REPORT-24H
