# Contractor Qualification Requirements

## 1. Purpose
To define qualification rules for contractor approval and onboarding.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all third-party contractors, subcontractors, and service vendors.

## 3. Core Information
Contractors must meet minimum experience and insurance requirements.
Contractors must pass CSO audit and sign required NDA version.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Minimum Experience: 5 years
Minimum Insurance Coverage (EUR): 2,000,000
Required NDA Version: NDA-6B
Audit Owner: Compliance & Safety Office (CSO)
