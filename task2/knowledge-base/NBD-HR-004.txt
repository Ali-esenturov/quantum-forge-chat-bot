# Workplace Conduct and Ethics Policy

## 1. Purpose
To define behavior standards and ethics reporting mechanisms.

## 2. Scope

## 3. Core Information
NBD enforces zero tolerance for harassment, bribery, and retaliation.
Ethics concerns can be raised via internal channels.
