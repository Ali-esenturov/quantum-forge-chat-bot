# Engineering Standards and Structural Requirements

## 1. Purpose
To define baseline engineering materials and structural performance requirements.

## 2. Scope
Applies to design approvals and material selection for NBD projects.

## 3. Core Information
NBD uses standardized concrete and steel grades for most projects.
Structural load tolerance must be verified during design and QA checks.

## 4. Key Metrics / Structured Data
Concrete Grade Standard: C35/45
Steel Grade Standard: S460
Maximum Load Tolerance (design reference): 450 kN
Minimum QA Inspection Frequency (structural): weekly

## 5. Reference Codes
ENG-C35-45
STEEL-S460
LOAD-450KN
